# Northwind shipping policy

Domestic US orders ship within 1–2 business days and usually arrive in 3–7 business days. A tracking link is emailed when the order ships.

International orders ship to the UK, EU, India, Canada, and Australia. Delivery typically takes 7–14 business days. The customer pays import duties.

For a shipment more than three business days past its estimate, open a carrier investigation. Offer replacement or refund once the carrier confirms loss, subject to approval policy.
