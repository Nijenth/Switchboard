# Northwind returns and refunds

Unworn items with tags may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Return shipping is free for US customers. Refunds go to the original payment method and always require an authorized reviewer.

Processing normally takes two business days after receipt. Banks typically take 5–10 business days to show a refund. Worn items, final-sale items, and returns after 30 days are ineligible for a standard refund. A manager may review store credit.
